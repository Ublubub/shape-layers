        Length Height Profile: depth rows Stretch

        Paint the wall's cross-section: left is the back, right is the front (room side). Each column of cells is one layer of depth, so you can step parts forward (plinths, beams, cornices) or set them back (panels). The stretch row repeats to fit any height, so the base stays on the floor and the beam stays at the top. Paint with a palette (P) to mix blocks with a gradient from bottom to top.

        Weights: how common each block is at the top and at the bottom of the wall. Edits apply to every wall using this palette.
